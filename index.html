Who We Are

The Straw Hats are a chill, friendly guild on Thunderlord focused on running Mythics, BGs, and Raids while keeping the vibes relaxed. Whether you're here to push content, returning to WoW after years away, or just want to hang out with good people — you'll fit right in.

Our Philosophy

We're here to vibe, have fun, and enjoy the game together. Every playstyle has a home on this ship. Treat your crewmates with respect, show up prepared, help when you can, and always come ready for the next adventure. Nakama above all.

What We Do

Weekly Normal and Heroic raid progression, organized Mythic+ key pushing, guild RBGs building conquest for arena teams, housing events, and regular social nights. Active Discord keeps every piece of the crew connected.

"Help each other out. We grow as a crew."
— The Straw Hat Code · Rule IV
⚓ The Code ⚓
01Be kind and chill. We're here to vibe, have fun, and enjoy the game. Treat everyone with respect.
02No drama. Take a breath, relax, and keep the guild a positive place for everyone.
03No advertising other guilds. Recruiting for other guilds in chat is not allowed.
04Help each other out. If someone needs advice, a dungeon run, or help with quests — lend a hand. We grow as a crew.
05No toxic behavior. No harassment, raging, griefing, or putting others down. Period.
06Keep guild chat clean-ish. Jokes and fun are welcome — just don't cross lines or make others uncomfortable.
07Show up for events you sign up for. If you can't make it, just let an officer know.
08No politics or religion discussions in guild chat or during events. Keep it about Azeroth.
09Respect officer calls during raids and group content. We're all here to succeed together.
10Most important: have fun, vibe, and enjoy the game with the Straw Hats. 🏴‍☠️

What We Look For

  • Heroic progression raiders should aim for 265+ item level — we're pushing into Heroic content
  • Alt Normal raids run regularly with no hard item level requirement — come as you are
  • Know your class rotation — come prepared, we trust you
  • Study boss mechanics before progression — Mythic Trap is guild-recommended
  • Show up for events you sign up for, or give an officer a heads up
  • Good attitude, positive energy, team-first mentality
  • Active in Discord — that's where everything lives
  • Casual players don't need raid requirements — all playstyles welcome
